## Business Model

Morgan Stanley เป็น global financial services firm ที่รายได้หลักมาจาก Institutional Securities, Wealth Management และ Investment Management. Thesis สำคัญคือ integrated firm: trading/IB ทำเงินดีใน active markets ขณะที่ Wealth Management ช่วยลด cyclicality และเพิ่ม fee-based asset gathering. Source anchor: [[MS_100526]].

## Segments

บริษัทมีสาม operating segments หลัก: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management และ Investment Management. Institutional Securities รวม investment banking, advisory, underwriting, equities, fixed income and prime brokerage; Wealth Management รวม advisors, fee-based assets, brokerage, lending, deposits and E*Trade-related channels. ## Competitive Position

MS is a higher-quality capital-markets and wealth-management financial than a pure investment bank, but still more cycle-sensitive than a consumer deposit bank. In the vault it sits beside [[JPM]] and [[BAC]]: JPM is the best-in-class diversified bank, BAC is the cheaper mega-bank route, and MS is the wealth/markets compounder with more valuation stretch after the rally. Main competitors include Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America/Merrill, UBS, Charles Schwab, BlackRock and private-credit/fintech platforms. Source anchor: [[MS_100526]].

## Moat

- Integrated institutional + wealth platform.
- Wealth net new asset and fee-based asset flow engine.
- Strong equities, fixed income, prime brokerage and investment-banking franchise.
- Global brand with offices in 42 countries.
- Strong capital base with Q1 2026 Standardized CET1 at 15.1%.
- Shareholder return through dividends and repurchases.

## Key Risks

- Valuation stretch near 52-week high and around 3.7x tangible book value.
- Markets revenue normalization after a very strong Q1 2026.
- IB cycle risk if M&A, IPOs, convertibles or debt issuance slow.
- Wealth fee sensitivity to market levels and net new asset flows.
- Expense discipline risk if compensation and non-comp costs rise faster than revenue.
- Capital-rule, SLR/eSLR, stress-capital and buyback constraints.
- CRE/credit provision tail risk.
- MS06 execution risk: D+N access helps, but live spread, premium and depth must be checked.

## Competitive Position

- Thailand: ThaiBev remains a leading beverage company, but I did not verify a current numerical 2025 market-share split from primary sources.
- Vietnam: Sabeco remains a major brewer, but accessible third-party data indicates market share pressure versus Heineken.
- NAB: competes with Osotspa, Carabao, Ichitan, TCP, Coca-Cola/Pepsi systems and local water/tea/energy brands.

## Moat

- Scale distribution across Thailand and ASEAN.
- Strong spirits and beer brands.
- Defensive consumption profile.
- Spirits profit pool supports group cash flow.
- Regional platform optionality through Sabeco, F&N, Oishi and food assets.

## Key Risks

- Revenue decline persists despite margin help.
- Beer/Vietnam/Sabeco weakness remains structural rather than cyclical.
- Net debt/EBITDA stays elevated.
- Dividend yield becomes less attractive after WHT, issuer fees or payout pressure.
- Thai alcohol regulation, excise, drink-driving enforcement and social/ESG exclusion cap the multiple.
- THAIBEV19 is D-only and can trade at a premium/discount versus Y92.SI fair value.

## Valuation Notes

Treat S$0.52-S$0.56 as a trim/review zone unless earnings upgrades arrive first.

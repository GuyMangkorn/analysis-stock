## Competitive Position

Meta เป็นหนึ่งใน digital ad platforms ใหญ่ที่สุดของโลก และ EMARKETER forecast ว่า Meta จะขึ้นเป็นอันดับ 1 ของ global digital ad net revenue ในปี 2026 ด้วย share ราว 26.8%. คู่แข่งหลักคือ Google/YouTube, TikTok/ByteDance, Amazon Ads, Snap, Pinterest, Reddit, X และ platform/privacy gatekeepers อย่าง Apple.

## Moat

- Network effects across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, Reels and Threads.
- Advertiser scale, conversion data, AI ad automation and creative tooling.
- Global distribution to billions of daily active people.
- Balance sheet and operating cash flow scale to fund AI capex.
- Messaging/social/video surfaces that can support personal AI and business agents.

## Key Risks

- FY2026 AI capex guidance $125B-$145B can compress FCF and buyback flexibility.
- Reality Labs loss remains a multibillion-dollar drag.
- Youth safety, privacy, antitrust and EU/U.S. regulatory remedies could change product design or increase compliance cost.
- Advertising demand is cyclical and sensitive to macro/commerce budgets.
- TikTok, YouTube, Amazon Ads and other platforms compete for attention and ad budgets.
- Insider activity remains mostly selling/planned selling rather than insider buying.

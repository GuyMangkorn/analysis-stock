## Competitive Position

Cisco remains a leading enterprise networking vendor. Latest report cites IDC data showing Cisco with 27.6% total Ethernet switch share and 30.6% router share in Q4 2025, plus 37.2% full-year 2025 enterprise WLAN share. Main competitors include Arista, Nvidia, HPE/Juniper, Huawei, Ubiquiti, Extreme Networks, Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet, CrowdStrike, Zscaler, Microsoft, Datadog and Dynatrace.

## Moat

- Large installed base across enterprise, public-sector and service-provider networks.
- Deep channel and partner ecosystem.
- End-to-end networking, security, observability and services portfolio.
- Silicon One and Nexus/8000 systems for AI networking.
- Services and RPO visibility.
- Splunk data/security/observability layer.

Moat pushback: Cisco's breadth is valuable, but AI data-center buyers can still choose Arista, Nvidia, Broadcom-based ODMs, HPE/Juniper or cloud self-build routes. Security and observability also face best-of-breed pressure.

## Competitive Position

JPM is the vault's first direct U.S. bank exposure. It is not comparable to Visa/Mastercard because JPM carries credit, capital and balance-sheet risk. The franchise quality comes from deposit scale, payments, CIB, markets, wealth/AUM and capital return. Source anchor: [[JPM_060526]].

## Moat

- Large, sticky deposit base and broad consumer/commercial relationships.
- Diversified revenue across CCB, CIB and AWM.
- Fortress balance sheet and strong CET1/liquidity profile.
- Payments, securities services, markets and investment-banking scale.
- AWM/private bank compounding potential from AUM, client assets and net inflows.

## Key Risks

- P/E looks low versus tech but JPM trades at a premium bank valuation on P/TBV.
- Credit-cycle risk, especially card net charge-offs and reserve builds.
- NII sensitivity to rates, deposit beta and balance-sheet mix.
- Basel/GSIB/RWA capital-rule risk that can lower buyback capacity.
- Markets and investment banking revenue normalization.
- Private credit or shadow-banking stress spilling into broader credit.
- Jamie Dimon succession confidence.
- No SET DR route; Thai investor access requires direct U.S. trading route.

## Competitive Position

NVIDIA is the leading listed pure-play route to AI accelerator, AI networking and full-stack accelerated computing demand. Latest full report treats current data-center AI accelerator market share as not verified from a primary source, but cites Jon Peddie Research via TechSpot for discrete desktop GPU share at 92% in Q3 2025. Main competitors include AMD, Google TPU, AWS Trainium/Inferentia, Intel Gaudi, custom hyperscaler ASICs, Broadcom, Arista, Cisco, Marvell and specialist AI chip vendors.

## Moat

- CUDA and developer ecosystem.
- Full-stack systems across GPU, networking, rack-scale systems and software.
- Blackwell/Rubin roadmap and supply allocation.
- Deep hyperscaler and AI model-maker customer relationships.
- High margins and cash generation to fund R&D, supply commitments and buybacks.

Moat pushback: customer concentration is material, hyperscalers have incentives to use custom silicon, China remains restricted/uncertain, and gross margin can compress if system mix or supply costs worsen.

## Key Risks

- Q1 FY2027 expectation bar is already high before the May 20 call.
- Valuation is stretched near a 52-week high.
- China/H200 approvals are not the same as confirmed deliveries or revenue.
- Two direct customers represented 22% and 14% of FY2026 revenue.
- Gross margin must remain around the 75% guide level.
- TSMC, HBM, advanced packaging, rack, power and data-center deployment timing are bottlenecks.
- Hyperscaler custom silicon can pressure long-term pricing and share.
- Insider selling optics are cautionary near highs.
- SET DR tracking, premium and spread must be checked live.

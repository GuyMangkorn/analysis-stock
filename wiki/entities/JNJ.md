## Competitive Position

JNJ is diversified across pharma and devices, so a single market-share number is not very useful. Company disclosures support that oncology is gaining share in several product lines, while the planned DePuy Synthes separation would create a leading orthopaedics-focused company. Exact, comparable percentage market share was not verified in the latest report.

| Area | Main competitors | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Oncology / multiple myeloma | Roche, AbbVie, Bristol Myers, Pfizer, Amgen, AstraZeneca, GSK | Strong growth from DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, ERLEADA, RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE |
| Immunology | AbbVie, Amgen, Novartis, Eli Lilly, UCB, Pfizer | TREMFYA/SIMPONI support, but STELARA biosimilars are a real drag |
| MedTech cardiovascular | Medtronic, Boston Scientific, Abbott, Edwards | EP, Abiomed, Shockwave are key growth drivers |
| Orthopaedics | Stryker, Zimmer Biomet, Smith+Nephew | DePuy Synthes separation planned |

## Moat

- Scale in global pharma R&D, clinical trials, regulatory affairs and commercialization.
- Oncology portfolio breadth, especially multiple myeloma and solid-tumor launches.
- MedTech installed base and surgeon/procedure relationships.
- Dividend record and balance-sheet credibility attract long-term defensive holders.
- Portfolio optionality from DePuy Synthes separation, if executed cleanly.

## Key Risks

- STELARA biosimilar erosion and future patent-cycle pressure.
- Talc/legal liabilities and reserve uncertainty.
- Valuation after a strong 12-month rally.
- Pipeline and launch execution must offset immunology erosion.
- MedTech margin/integration risk after Abiomed/Shockwave and before DePuy separation.
- `JNJ03` DR liquidity/spread/premium risk despite D+N session.

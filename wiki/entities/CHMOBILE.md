## Business Model

China Mobile provides communications, computing, and AI services in Mainland China and Hong Kong. Core communications remains the cash engine, while computing services, China Mobile Cloud, AIDC, data centers, and AI services are the growth layer.

## Competitive Position

China Mobile remains the dominant Chinese mobile operator by subscriber scale. Q1 2026 data showed nearly 1.009B mobile customers and 668M 5G network customers, clearly ahead of China Telecom by mobile subscriber base. China Unicom disclosure is less directly comparable because it reports aggregate connectivity subscribers.

## Moat

- Massive mobile, broadband, IoT and 5G network scale.
- State-backed role in telecom, computing network, AI infrastructure and future 6G development.
- Balance sheet strength and dividend credibility.
- Ability to cross-sell cloud, AI, AIDC and enterprise services into an existing government/enterprise base.

## Key Risks

- Core communications revenue is mature and declined in FY2025.
- Q1 2026 profit and EPS declined YoY despite revenue growth.
- VAT policy changes may pressure margins.
- AI/compute capex must earn returns, not just serve national strategy.
- SOE capital allocation may not always prioritize minority shareholders.
- CEO transition after He Biao's February 2026 reassignment deserves monitoring.
- CHMOBILE DRs are daytime-only and can trade with wider spread than the HK underlying.

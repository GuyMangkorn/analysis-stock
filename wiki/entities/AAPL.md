## Business Model

Apple ทำเงินจาก iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables/Home/Accessories และ Services เช่น App Store, AppleCare, iCloud, Apple Pay, Apple Music, Apple TV และ subscription bundle. Services เป็น margin engine เพราะ Q2 FY2026 Services gross margin อยู่ที่ 76.7% เทียบกับ Products gross margin 38.7% <cite>[Apple Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000013/aapl-20260328.htm)</cite>.

## Competitive Position

Counterpoint Research ระบุว่า Apple ได้ 21% global smartphone shipment share ใน Q1 2026, โต 5% YoY, และเป็นอันดับ 1 ของ Q1 เป็นครั้งแรกในข้อมูลของ Counterpoint; Samsung อยู่ที่ 20%, Xiaomi 12%, OPPO 11%, vivo 8% <cite>[Counterpoint Research Japan Q1 2026 global smartphone shipments](https://japan.counterpointresearch.com/insights/global-smartphone-shipments-q1-2026/)</cite>. ตัวเลขนี้เป็น shipment share แบบ preliminary / sell-in ไม่ใช่ revenue share หรือ installed-base share.

## Moat

- Installed base and ecosystem lock-in across iPhone, Mac, iPad, Watch, AirPods, Services and App Store.
- Vertical integration across Apple silicon, hardware, OS, retail and services.
- Brand/pricing power that supports both premium products and new lower-entry products such as iPhone 17e and MacBook Neo.
- High-margin Services layer.
- Capital return through buybacks; Apple authorized up to $100B additional repurchases after Q2 FY2026 <cite>[Apple Q2 FY2026 results](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/04/apple-reports-second-quarter-results/)</cite>.

## Key Risks

- Valuation is stretched near $300: current P/E around mid-30s and consensus target upside near 2%.
- iPhone remains the biggest revenue line, so product-cycle disappointment still matters.
- App Store / link-out regulation can pressure Services economics.
- Component, memory, tariff and supply-chain risks can pressure gross margin.
- AI feature quality, security and product-defect risk can hurt reputation.
- CEO transition: Tim Cook becomes Executive Chairman and John Ternus becomes CEO effective 1 Sep 2026 <cite>[Apple CEO transition release](https://www.apple.com/ca/newsroom/2026/04/tim-cook-to-become-apple-executive-chairman-john-ternus-to-become-apple-ceo/)</cite>.

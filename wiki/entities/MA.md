## Business Model

Mastercard เป็น global payments technology network ไม่ใช่ธนาคาร: บริษัทไม่ออกบัตร, ไม่ปล่อยกู้ และไม่รับ cardholder credit risk โดยตรง. รายได้มาจาก payment network fees tied to GDV/cross-border/switched transactions และ Value-added services and solutions เช่น security, digital/authentication, business insights, consulting, loyalty, processing/gateway และ account-based payment solutions.

## Segments

บริษัทมี one reportable operating segment คือ **Payment Solutions**. สำหรับการวิเคราะห์ ใช้ revenue source และ geography mix แทน segment.

## Competitive Position

Mastercard เป็น global card-network #2 เมื่อเทียบกับ Visa ใน U.S. purchase volume. Nilson 2025 data implies Mastercard has roughly 25.8% share of the U.S. four-network purchase-volume pool and roughly 29.6% share of the Visa+Mastercard pool. Visa มี scale edge, ส่วน MA มี VAS growth และ commercial/digital/security optionality ที่น่าสนใจ.

Competitors and threats include Visa, American Express, Discover/Capital One, PayPal, Apple Pay/digital wallets, ACH/RTP/FedNow, Pix/UPI, stablecoins, CBDC and government-backed digital public infrastructure.

## Moat

- Two-sided global acceptance network with high integration/switching cost.
- Trusted brand and settlement/rules infrastructure for cross-border commerce.
- Security, fraud, tokenization, authentication and data products that attach to core network flows.
- High operating leverage: Q1 2026 adjusted operating margin was 60.8%.
- Buyback-supported per-share compounding, though buyback should not be used to justify chasing valuation.
- Optionality in agentic commerce, Mastercard Move, commercial payments and planned BVNK stablecoin infrastructure acquisition.

## Key Risks

- Valuation de-rating if EPS growth slows or P/E compresses toward low-20s.
- Interchange, routing, antitrust, surcharging, privacy/data/AI, AML/sanctions and digital-economy regulation.
- A2A/RTP/stablecoin/wallet disruption, especially in domestic debit and merchant-initiated flows.
- Cross-border/travel sensitivity from geopolitical or macro shocks.
- Payment network rebates/incentives growing faster than revenue.
- BVNK/stablecoin execution and regulatory risk.
- MA80 DR tracking, premium/discount and spread risk.

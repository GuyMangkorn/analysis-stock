## Moat

- EDLP trust and purchasing scale.
- U.S. grocery traffic frequency.
- Store-fulfilled omnichannel network.
- Retail media and membership profit pools.
- Private brands such as Great Value and Member's Mark.
- Supply-chain investments and owned food-processing capacity.
- Strong cash flow to fund capex, dividends and buybacks.

## Key Risks

- Valuation compression from ~45x forward P/E toward historical norms.
- U.S. grocery share erosion or weak traffic.
- Tariffs, labor, claims, shrink and freight/energy pressure.
- Low-margin grocery mix if ads, membership and marketplace growth slows.
- Amazon, Costco, Aldi and Target competitive pressure.
- International FX/market-mix risk.
- `WMT06` spread, volume, premium/discount and issuer process risk.

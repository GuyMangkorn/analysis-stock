## Competitive Position

BAC is the vault's second direct U.S. bank after [[JPM]]. It is cheaper than JPM on P/TBV and P/E, but its Q1 2026 ROTCE was lower than JPM's, so it deserves a lower premium unless NII, deposits, credit and capital return keep improving. Main competitors are JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo, Citigroup, Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, UBS, Charles Schwab, Capital One and American Express. Source anchor: [[BAC_090526]].

## Moat

- Massive U.S. deposit and consumer checking-account base.
- Merrill and Private Bank wealth-management scale.
- Corporate, commercial and treasury-services client relationships.
- Global Markets franchise that can monetize volatility and client activity.
- Digital banking scale across mobile, online, Erica and Zelle usage.
- Capital return through dividends and repurchases when CET1 and RWA allow it.

## Key Risks

- Credit cycle, especially consumer credit-card charge-offs and reserve builds.
- NII sensitivity to rates, deposit pricing, fixed-rate asset repricing and balance-sheet mix.
- CET1/RWA and capital-rule risk that can reduce buyback capacity.
- Global Markets and IB revenue normalization after strong Q1 2026 client activity.
- Wealth fees sensitive to market levels and advisor/client flows.
- Litigation, Zelle/payment fraud, AML/sanctions, interchange and broader regulatory overhang.
- BAC03 execution risk: D+N access is useful, but snapshot spread was wide and volume low.

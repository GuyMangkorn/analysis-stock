## Competitive Position

CrowdStrike is a leading endpoint / XDR / cybersecurity platform vendor. Latest report cites the 2025 Gartner EPP Leader announcement and maps main competitors as Microsoft Defender, Palo Alto Networks Cortex, SentinelOne, Trend Micro, Trellix and adjacent broader security platforms. Current 2026 independent market-share percentage could not be verified from accessible sources; older IDC data cited by CrowdStrike from 2021 is treated as stale, not current.

## Moat

- Cloud-native Falcon architecture and single-agent platform.
- Large endpoint telemetry base and threat intelligence.
- Subscription-heavy revenue model.
- Platform consolidation story across endpoint, cloud, identity, data, SIEM and AI-security.
- Partner/channel motion and marketplace integrations.
- FCF generation despite GAAP losses.

Moat pushback: Microsoft has bundled distribution; Palo Alto has platform breadth; SentinelOne remains a credible endpoint-native competitor; AI-native security tools can pressure pricing if customers see enough good-enough automation.

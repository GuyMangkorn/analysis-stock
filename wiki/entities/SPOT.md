## Business Model

Spotify reports two segments: Premium and Ad-Supported. Premium revenue is generated mainly from subscription fees, while Ad-Supported revenue comes from advertising across music and podcast content <cite>[Spotify 2025 Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639920/000162828026006874/ck0001639920-20251231.htm)</cite>.

## Segments

| Segment | Role |
|---|---|
| Premium | Main revenue and gross profit engine; subscription access to music, podcasts, audiobooks and video-related experiences |
| Ad-Supported | Free user funnel and ad monetization layer; lower-margin but important for acquisition and conversion |

## Competitive Position

Spotify is a global leader in streaming audio with 761M MAU and 293M Premium subscribers in Q1 2026 <cite>[Spotify Q1 2026 6-K / shareholder update](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639920/000114036126017211/ef20071303_ex99-1.htm)</cite>. Main competitors include Apple Music, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, Deezer, Joox, Pandora, SoundCloud, podcast platforms, audiobook platforms and large advertising platforms <cite>[Spotify 2025 Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639920/000162828026006874/ck0001639920-20251231.htm)</cite>.

## Moat

Spotify's moat คือ **scale + personalization/data loop + habit/switching cost + creator/advertiser marketplace**:

| Moat layer | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Scale | 761M MAU creates a large listening/taste graph and strengthens discovery products <cite>[Spotify Q1 2026 6-K / shareholder update](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639920/000114036126017211/ef20071303_ex99-1.htm)</cite> |
| Personalization | Taste Profile, Prompted Playlist, SongDNA and About the Song turn listening data into user control and engagement <cite>[Spotify Q1 2026 6-K / shareholder update](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639920/000114036126017211/ef20071303_ex99-1.htm)</cite> |
| Habit / switching cost | Playlists, follows, listening history, recommendations and Wrapped identity make switching inconvenient |
| Creator relationship | Spotify said it paid the music industry more than $11B in 2025 and nearly $70B all-time <cite>[Spotify Q1 2026 newsroom release](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-04-28/spotify-q1-2026-earnings/)</cite> |

Pushback: moat นี้ไม่แข็งแบบ infrastructure monopoly. Spotify depends on third-party licenses for most streamed content, and concentrated content providers can affect access and terms <cite>[Spotify 2025 Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639920/000162828026006874/ck0001639920-20251231.htm)</cite>.

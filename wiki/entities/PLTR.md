## Competitive Position

Market share could not be verified from a credible standardized source. The practical competitor set includes large enterprise software vendors, government contractors, system integrators, hyperscalers, model/platform vendors and internal customer builds. PLTR's edge is operational deployment depth, not a generic chatbot or BI dashboard.

## Moat

- Mission-critical government workflows.
- Ontology and permission/action model.
- AIP adoption loop across commercial and government customers.
- Forward-deployed engineering and customer-near implementation.
- Strong customer expansion, net dollar retention and deal velocity.
- $8.0B cash/Treasuries and no debt at Q1 2026.

Moat pushback: large enterprise software companies and hyperscalers can bundle AI/data workflows into existing customer relationships. PLTR must keep proving that AIP delivers operational value that customers cannot easily rebuild or replace.

## Key Risks

- Valuation is still extreme after the correction.
- Great results can still produce stock weakness if expectations are too high.
- Government/political/privacy risk is structural, not temporary.
- Long enterprise/government sales cycles can make quarterly results uneven.
- Insider selling-plan optics are not supportive of aggressive buying.
- Stock-based compensation and dilution need ongoing monitoring.

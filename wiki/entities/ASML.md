ASML is a semiconductor equipment chokepoint with EUV leadership, strong AI infrastructure demand, and high installed-base value. The concern is not business quality; the concern is valuation after a sharp rally.

## Business Model

ASML sells lithography systems, metrology/inspection tools, computational lithography, and installed-base services/upgrades to chipmakers. The core economics come from EUV leadership plus long-lived service and productivity upgrades once tools are installed in fabs.

## Segments

| Segment | Role |
|---|---|
| EUV | Leading-edge logic and advanced DRAM enabler; highest moat. |
| DUV | Mature/mainstream and China-exposed demand; useful but more cyclical/policy-sensitive. |
| Metrology & Inspection | Yield and overlay control. |
| Installed Base Management | Services, field options, upgrades; attractive recurring-like support layer. |

## Moat

- EUV technology lead and ecosystem lock-in are very strong.
- Customer roadmaps depend on ASML coordination years ahead.
- Installed-base services deepen switching costs.
- R&D scale supports the gap, but the same complexity creates supply-chain and geopolitical risk.

## Key Risks

- Valuation compression from ~50x TTM P/E toward historical median.
- Export controls and China DUV normalization.
- High-NA EUV adoption timing risk if key customers delay because economics are not ready.
- Semiconductor capex cyclicality and customer concentration.
- Thai investor execution risk through ASML01 spread, FX, and DR liquidity.

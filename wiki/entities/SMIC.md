## Moat

- Scarce domestic advanced foundry capacity in China.
- Policy and strategic support from China localization cycle.
- Customer proximity to Chinese fabless/system companies.
- Strategic Huawei link through teardown/industry reporting.

Moat is real but policy-heavy. It can rerate quickly when China AI/self-reliance sentiment rises, and derate quickly when sanctions or yield risk returns.

## Key Risks

- Valuation: P/E near 100x and forward P/E near 68x in latest snapshot.
- High capex and negative FCF pressure.
- Advanced-node yield uncertainty.
- Sanctions risk across DUV service, EDA, HBM, materials, and packaging.
- Huawei exposure is strategically important but not quantified by company disclosure.
- Thai DR liquidity/spread and day-session-only trading.

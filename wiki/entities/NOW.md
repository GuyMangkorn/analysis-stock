## Business Model

ServiceNow sells subscription software for digital workflows across enterprise functions. The key economic engine is cloud/subscription revenue, supported by implementation, training, and professional services.

Main monetization:

| Engine | Role |
|---|---|
| Technology workflows | ITSM, ITOM, asset management, risk, operational technology, security operations |
| CRM and Industry | Customer service, field service, sales/order management, industry-specific workflows |
| Core Business | HR, workplace, legal, finance, facilities workflows |
| Creator and Other | Low-code/no-code, app development, platform extensibility |
| AI / Now Assist | AI agents, workflow automation, data fabric, contextual enterprise AI |

## Competitive Position

ServiceNow is positioned as an enterprise workflow orchestration layer. The company benefits from switching costs, integration depth, workflow data, and mission-critical process footprint.

Competitive sets include Salesforce, Microsoft, Oracle, SAP, Workday, Atlassian, BMC, Freshworks, Zendesk-like service tools, AI-native app builders, and systems integrators.

Exact market share has not been verified from a credible current source. Treat analyst "Leader" positioning as supportive evidence, not market-share proof.

## Moat

- High subscription mix and enterprise contract structure.
- Workflow depth across IT, customer service, HR, risk, field service, and industry operations.
- Cross-system integration and governance layer that AI tools still need for enterprise execution.
- Large-customer expansion: Q1 2026 ended with 630 customers above $5M ACV.
- AI optionality through Now Assist, AI agents, Workflow Data Fabric, and Moveworks integration.

## Key Risks

1. AI-native tools reduce need for expensive workflow/platform seats.
2. Q2/FY2026 cRPO slows below high-teens.
3. Armis/Moveworks integration drags margin or distracts execution.
4. Large systems-of-record vendors bundle workflow and AI features.
5. Insider selling and weak software sentiment keep multiple compressed.
6. SET DR liquidity/spread creates execution risk for Thai investors.

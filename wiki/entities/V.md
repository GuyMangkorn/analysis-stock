## Business Model

Visa เป็น global payments network ไม่ใช่ธนาคาร: บริษัทไม่ออกบัตร, ไม่ปล่อยกู้ และไม่รับ credit risk ผู้ถือบัตรโดยตรง. รายได้มาจาก service revenue, data processing, international transaction revenue, other/value-added services และหักด้วย client incentives.

## Segments

Visa รายงานเป็น one reportable segment: **Payment Services**. สำหรับการวิเคราะห์ ใช้ revenue category และ geography แทน segment.

## Customer And Revenue Trend

- Net revenue compounded from $32.653B in FY2023 to $40.000B in FY2025, about 10.7% CAGR.
- H1 FY2026 net revenue was $22.131B, up about 15.8% YoY.
- FY2025 network scale: nearly 14,500 financial institution clients, nearly 5B payment credentials, and 175M+ merchant locations.
- Long-term trend is still cash-to-digital, contactless, ecommerce, cross-border, commercial/money movement and VAS attach.

## Competitive Position

Visa is one of the largest global card networks. Nilson data for H1 2025 shows Visa at 38.47% share of global brand card purchase transactions. In U.S. purchase volume, Visa's 2025 purchase volume of $7.028T implies roughly 61% share versus the $11.463T U.S. global brand card purchase-volume pool.

Key competitors and threats: Mastercard, American Express, Discover/Capital One, UnionPay, JCB, PayPal, Apple Pay, digital wallets, domestic schemes, A2A/RTP networks, stablecoins and merchant/regulatory routing pressure.

## Moat

- Global acceptance network and two-sided scale.
- Bank/client integration and issuer/acquirer relationships.
- Transaction data, fraud/risk models, tokenization and authorization infrastructure.
- Cross-border acceptance and currency-conversion economics.
- Value-added services revenue, which reached $10.9B in FY2025.
- High operating leverage and asset-light model without direct cardholder credit risk.

## Key Risks

- Interchange, routing, antitrust and payment regulation.
- Client incentives growing faster than revenue.
- A2A/RTP/stablecoin disruption in selected use cases.
- Cross-border/travel macro sensitivity.
- Cybersecurity and payment-system resilience.
- Valuation de-rating if EPS growth normalizes.
- SET DR tracking, premium/discount and spread risk.

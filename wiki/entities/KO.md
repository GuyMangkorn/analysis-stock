## Moat

- Global brand salience across Coca-Cola, Sprite, Fanta and other beverages.
- Asset-light concentrate economics with high-margin royalty-like characteristics.
- Massive bottling and distribution network.
- Category breadth across sparkling, hydration, sports, coffee, tea, juice/dairy and energy.
- Pricing power, shown by Q1 2026 organic revenue growth with positive volume.
- Dividend identity and defensive investor base.

## Key Risks

- Valuation compression from ~24x-25x earnings toward 20x-22x.
- Volume elasticity if price/mix runs ahead of consumer tolerance.
- FX headwinds because 60%+ of revenue is international.
- Sugar taxes, plastic/package regulation, labeling and health concerns.
- Bottler execution and refranchising/reporting noise.
- Commodity and packaging cost pressure.
- `KO80` spread, premium/discount and issuer process risk.

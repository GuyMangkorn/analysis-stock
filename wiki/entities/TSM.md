## Business Model

TSMC ผลิต semiconductor ให้ลูกค้าโดยไม่ออกแบบ/ขาย chip ภายใต้แบรนด์ตัวเอง ทำให้ลูกค้า fabless/IDM ไว้ใจใช้ TSMC เป็น manufacturing partner ระยะยาว. รายได้หลักมาจาก wafer fabrication บน advanced nodes, specialty nodes และ advanced packaging.

| Engine | Role |

| Smartphone | Mobile SoCs and application processors |
| Advanced nodes | 3nm, 5nm, 7nm, N2/N2P/A16 roadmap |
| Advanced packaging | CoWoS, InFO, SoIC, 3DFabric |
| Global manufacturing | Taiwan core plus Arizona, Japan, Germany expansion |

## Competitive Position

TrendForce reports TSMC held 70.4% share of global top-10 foundry revenue in 4Q25. Main competitors include Samsung Foundry, SMIC, UMC, GlobalFoundries, HuaHong, Tower, Vanguard, Nexchip, and PSMC. TSMC's advantage is strongest in leading-edge process, scale, customer trust, and advanced packaging.

## Moat

- Pure-play trust: TSMC does not compete with customers by selling its own chips.
- Process leadership: 3nm/5nm/7nm collectively dominate wafer revenue.
- Scale: 534 customers and 12,682 products in 2025.
- Advanced packaging bottleneck: CoWoS/SoIC/3DFabric attach to AI demand.
- Balance sheet: large net cash position and positive FCF even during heavy capex.

## Key Risks

- Valuation compression after an extended rally.
- AI capex overbuild or hyperscaler order slowdown.
- Taiwan Strait / US-China export-control risk.
- Capex and depreciation pressure from global fab expansion.
- N2/N2P/A16/A14 execution risk.
- North America customer concentration.
- USD/THB, TWD/USD, ADR dividend withholding, and broker execution costs.

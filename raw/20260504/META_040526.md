## Key Risks

1. **AI capex overbuild:** FY2026 capex guidance $125B-$145B can compress FCF and buyback flexibility.
2. **Reality Labs drag:** RL loss remains multibillion-dollar per quarter and FY2025 loss was $19.193B.
3. **Regulatory/legal remedy:** New Mexico, EU DSA/youth safety, privacy, antitrust, and app-store/platform control can raise compliance cost or reduce engagement.
4. **Ad cyclicality:** Commerce, SMB budgets, macro shocks and FX can hit ad pricing.
5. **Competition for attention:** TikTok/YouTube/Amazon ads can pressure time spent and ad wallet share.
6. **User engagement quality:** DAP +4% YoY is good, but Q1 saw a QoQ DAP decline from Iran/Russia disruptions; watch if this becomes broader.
7. **Insider selling optics:** planned selling is normal, but no insider buying signal at current levels.

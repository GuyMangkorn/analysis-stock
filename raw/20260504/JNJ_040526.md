## 2. Position in Cycle

**Cycle pattern: Extended Rally.**

JNJ is not in structural decline. Q1 numbers are solid, FY2026 guidance was raised, oncology is working, MedTech growth is reasonable, and the dividend remains high quality. But the stock already rerated hard: latest close $227.19 is ~+47% over roughly 12 months from 1 May 2025 and only ~9.7% below the 52-week high <cite>[FinanceCharts JNJ price](https://www.financecharts.com/stocks/JNJ/summary/price)</cite>.

The key pushback: at ~$227, the market is paying close to 20x FY2026 adjusted EPS for a company guiding roughly 7% reported sales growth and 7.1% adjusted EPS growth at the midpoint <cite>[JNJ Q1 2026 results](https://www.investor.jnj.com/investor-news/news-details/2026/Johnson--Johnson-reports-Q1-2026-results-raises-2026-outlook/)</cite>. That is not absurd, but it leaves little room for:

| Risk | Valuation implication |
|---|---|
| STELARA decline worse than offset from oncology/Neuroscience | EPS multiple compresses toward 17x-18x |
| Talc/legal headline or reserve change | Defensive multiple gets discounted again |
| MedTech margin/integration disappointment | DePuy separation story loses credibility |
| Analyst targets reset lower after rally | Stock can drift despite decent earnings |

So the correct stance is not "avoid JNJ"; it is **do not pay momentum price for a defensive stock**. Defensive quality is valuable only when the entry gives a margin of safety.

### Dividend / Tax Note

JNJ's new indicated annual dividend is **$5.36/share** after the Apr 2026 dividend increase <cite>[Nasdaq/JNJ dividend release](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/johnson-johnson-announces-64th-consecutive-year-dividend-increase-raises-quarterly)</cite>. At $227.19, gross yield is about **2.36%**. The U.S.-Thailand treaty generally caps U.S. withholding tax on dividends to Thai resident beneficial owners at **15%** in non-control cases <cite>[U.S.-Thailand tax treaty](https://www.stayexempt.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/thailand.pdf)</cite>. Net after 15% withholding is roughly:

```text
gross yield = $5.36 / $227.19 = 2.36%
net yield after 15% U.S. WHT ~= 2.36% x 85% = 2.01%
```

ดังนั้น JNJ เป็น income support ได้ แต่ไม่ใช่ dividend-yield bargain. ถ้าซื้อผ่าน DR ให้เช็ก issuer process, fees, FX treatment และ tax document flow เพิ่มเติม.

| Portfolio discipline | Orthopaedics separation could improve growth/margin mix, but only after execution proof |

## Key Risks

1. **STELARA cliff / biosimilars:** STELARA FY2025 sales fell from $10.361B to $6.078B, and JNJ expects continued global biosimilar launches to keep pressuring sales <cite>[JNJ 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000016/jnj-20251228.htm)</cite>.
2. **Talc/legal reserve risk:** Legal outcomes can distort GAAP earnings, cash flow perception and multiple.
3. **Valuation after rally:** A defensive stock up ~47% in 12 months has less room for error.
4. **Pipeline execution:** Oncology momentum must keep offsetting immunology erosion.
5. **MedTech integration:** Abiomed/Shockwave growth is positive, but acquisition integration and procedure demand must sustain.
6. **Orthopaedics separation:** Could unlock value, but separation costs, margin dis-synergies, tax structure, and investor perception can create volatility.
7. **Dividend not high enough alone:** Net yield near 2% after U.S. WHT is useful but not a standalone buy reason.
8. **DR execution:** `JNJ03` has D+N session, but small local order book can still create spread/indicative-value risk.

### Latest Results / Guidance

FY2026 was strong. Walmart reported total revenue of **$713.2B**, up **4.7%**, operating income up **1.6%**, global eCommerce sales up **24%**, global advertising business up **46%** to nearly **$6.4B**, operating cash flow of **$41.6B**, free cash flow of **$14.9B**, and an annual dividend increase to **$0.99/share** <cite>[Walmart Q4 FY26 earnings release](https://corporate.walmart.com/content/dam/corporate/documents/newsroom/2026/02/19/walmart-releases-q4-fy26-earnings/q4-fy26-earnings-release.pdf)</cite>.

Q4 FY2026 also showed business mix improvement: revenue grew **5.6%**, global eCommerce grew **24%**, Walmart U.S. eCommerce grew **27%**, Sam's Club U.S. eCommerce grew **23%**, Walmart International eCommerce grew **17%**, global advertising grew **37%**, and membership fee revenue grew **15.1%** <cite>[Walmart Q4 FY26 earnings release](https://corporate.walmart.com/content/dam/corporate/documents/newsroom/2026/02/19/walmart-releases-q4-fy26-earnings/q4-fy26-earnings-release.pdf)</cite>.

The FY2027 guide is solid but not explosive. Management guided FY2027 constant-currency net sales growth of **3.5%-4.5%**, adjusted operating income growth of **6.0%-8.0%**, adjusted EPS of **$2.75-$2.85**, and capex at about **3.5% of net sales** <cite>[Walmart Q4 FY26 earnings release](https://corporate.walmart.com/content/dam/corporate/documents/newsroom/2026/02/19/walmart-releases-q4-fy26-earnings/q4-fy26-earnings-release.pdf)</cite>. That supports the business quality thesis, but it does not automatically justify buying above 45x forward earnings.

### Competitive Position / Market Share

Walmart's clearest market-share advantage is U.S. grocery. Grocery Dive, citing Numerator data, reported that Walmart held **19.9% U.S. grocery market share** for the 12 months ending 31 March 2026, down slightly from **20.0%** in 2025 and **20.4%** in 2024; Kroger and Costco were each just above **8%** <cite>[Grocery Dive on Walmart grocery share](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/walmarts-grocery-sales-growth-slows/818493/)</cite>. Supermarket News reported the same Numerator share path and ranked Walmart as the grocery-market leader despite the slight decline <cite>[Supermarket News on Numerator grocery share](https://www.supermarketnews.com/grocery-trends-data/walmart-continues-to-lose-grocery-market-share)</cite>.

| Market / category | Main competitors | WMT position |
|---|---|---|
| U.S. grocery / food & beverage | Kroger, Costco, Albertsons, Publix, Aldi, Amazon/Whole Foods, Target | Leader by share, but share is edging down; grocery scale anchors traffic |
| Value retail / mass merchant | Target, Amazon, Costco, Dollar General, Dollar Tree, Aldi | Walmart has price leadership and omnichannel density; Target has more discretionary style exposure; Costco has membership loyalty but smaller assortment |
| U.S. eCommerce / marketplace | Amazon, Target, Costco, Instacart, grocery delivery platforms | Walmart's edge is store-fulfilled pickup/delivery; Amazon remains the broader eCommerce benchmark |
| Warehouse club | Costco, BJ's, Amazon/retail memberships | Sam's Club has strong grocery and membership momentum, but Costco remains the premium club comp |
| Retail media / ads | Amazon Ads, Instacart Ads, Target Roundel, Kroger Precision Marketing | Walmart Connect is scaling fast from a smaller base; growth is important because ads are higher-margin than grocery |

Recent initiatives support the moat but are not enough to erase valuation risk. Walmart announced the first full refresh of Great Value in more than a decade, covering almost **10,000** food and consumables items over two years; Walmart described Great Value as its largest private brand and the largest food-and-consumables CPG brand in the U.S. <cite>[Walmart Great Value redesign](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/04/15/walmart-unveils-modern-redesign-of-great-value-its-flagship-private-brand)</cite>. Walmart also opened a third owned-and-operated milk processing facility in Robinson, Texas, a more than **$350M** investment that will supply more than **650** Walmart stores and Sam's Clubs in the South Central U.S. <cite>[Walmart Robinson milk facility](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/04/29/walmart-opens-third-owned-and-operated-milk-processing-facility-in-robinson-texas-creating-more-than-400-new-jobs)</cite>.

Risk/reward:

```text
risk/reward to reasonable bull = upside to $152.50 / downside to $102.60
                             = 16.7% / 21.5%
                             = 0.78x

risk/reward to consensus target = upside to $133.86 / downside to $102.60
                                = 2.5% / 21.5%
                                = 0.12x
```

This is the key: the business is better than average, but the entry is not. A 0.78x bull/miss setup is not attractive for fresh money. At the preferred starter zone **$112-$118**, the same $152.50 bull case gives +29% to +36% upside, while the miss case at $102.60 gives about -8% to -13% downside. That is the kind of entry where defensive quality starts to work for Mangkorn instead of against him.

|---|---|---|
| Walmart U.S. stores | Core traffic, grocery, pharmacy, general merchandise, pickup/delivery footprint | Highest strategic value; 68% of net sales |
| Sam's Club U.S. | Membership warehouse club, grocery-heavy, fuel and general merchandise | Defensive traffic and membership economics |
| Walmart International | Mexico/Central America, China, Canada, Flipkart and other markets | Growth but FX, format and market-mix risk |
| eCommerce / omnichannel | Digital orders fulfilled by stores/clubs plus ecosystem offerings | Key moat against Amazon and delivery platforms |
| Advertising / Walmart Connect | Retail media monetization using shopper data | Higher-margin growth engine |
| Membership / Walmart+ / Sam's | Loyalty, delivery economics, recurring fee income | Improves frequency and profit mix |
| Supply chain / owned processing | Dairy, beef, automation, fulfillment, local sourcing | Supports price leadership and in-stock reliability |

## Moat

- **Scale and EDLP trust:** Walmart's Everyday Low Price model is still the heart of the moat, especially in grocery and consumables.
- **Store-fulfilled omnichannel density:** Over 8,400 global pickup/delivery locations make store-based fulfillment a real advantage <cite>[Walmart FY2026 Form 10-K](https://stock.walmart.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000104169-26-000055/wmt-20260131.htm)</cite>.
- **Grocery traffic frequency:** Grocery is 59.1% of Walmart U.S. sales and 69.6% of Sam's Club U.S. sales, making demand more recurring than discretionary retail.
- **Retail media data loop:** Walmart Connect can monetize shopper intent and closed-loop attribution.
- **Membership flywheel:** Walmart+ and Sam's Club membership fee growth improve loyalty and margin mix.
- **Supply-chain control:** Owned milk/beef facilities, automation and scale purchasing help Walmart defend price gaps.
- **Financial capacity:** Cash flow supports capex, dividends, buybacks and technology investment.

## Key Risks

1. **Valuation compression:** A move from 45x forward P/E toward even 38x can erase a large part of capital value without a business collapse.
2. **Grocery share erosion:** Walmart is still the leader, but Numerator data shows slight share decline; that matters because grocery is the traffic engine.
3. **Tariffs and trade policy:** Walmart warned that tariffs/trade policies are among the factors that can materially affect future results <cite>[Walmart Q4 FY26 earnings release](https://corporate.walmart.com/content/dam/corporate/documents/newsroom/2026/02/19/walmart-releases-q4-fy26-earnings/q4-fy26-earnings-release.pdf)</cite>.
4. **Labor, claims and operating expense pressure:** FY2026 operating expense ratio increased partly from U.S. self-insured general liability claims and depreciation from capital investments <cite>[Walmart FY2026 Form 10-K](https://stock.walmart.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000104169-26-000055/wmt-20260131.htm)</cite>.
5. **Low-margin business mix:** Grocery defends traffic but can cap profitability if high-margin growth slows.
6. **Amazon/Costco/Aldi competition:** Amazon pushes delivery and ads, Costco has membership loyalty, Aldi pressures grocery price gaps.
7. **International and FX risk:** Walmart International grew but currency and market mix can obscure progress.
8. **DR execution risk:** `WMT06` has D+N session, but snapshot volume was modest; use limit orders and check tracking.

## Verdict

Walmart เป็น **quality defensive retailer** ที่กำลังมี profit-mix optionality จาก eCommerce, ads, membership, marketplace และ supply-chain automation. ถ้าดูแค่ business quality, WMT น่ามีที่ใน watchlist/core候補 สำหรับนักลงทุนไทยที่ต้องการ defensive U.S. consumer exposure และมี SET DR route.

แต่การลงทุนไม่ใช่การซื้อบริษัทดีในราคาอะไรก็ได้. ที่ราว **$130**, upside ถึง consensus แทบไม่เหลือ และ stress test ให้ risk/reward ต่ำกว่า 1x. สำหรับ Mangkorn: **Hold if owned; fresh money wait.** เริ่มซื้อเพียง **10%-15% starter ที่ $112-$118** หรือ WMT06 equivalent หลังเช็ก live spread/indicative value. เพิ่มเฉพาะที่ **$100-$108** ถ้า Q1/Q2 FY2027 ยืนยันว่า traffic, grocery share, eCommerce economics, ads/membership และ margin leverage ยังดีจริง.

Coca-Cola reported **net revenues up 12% to $12.5B**, **organic revenues up 10%**, **unit case volume up 3%**, operating margin **32.9%**, comparable operating margin **33.8%**, GAAP EPS **$0.91** up 18%, and comparable EPS **$0.86** up 18% <cite>[Coca-Cola Q1 2026 results](https://investors.coca-colacompany.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1158/coca-cola-reports-first-quarter-2026-results-and-updates-full-year-guidance)</cite>.

Management updated full-year guidance: organic revenue growth remains **4%-5%**, comparable currency-neutral EPS growth remains **8%-9%**, but comparable EPS growth is now expected at **8%-9%** versus prior **3% down to 1% down**, largely due to foreign-currency and CCBA refranchising impacts <cite>[Coca-Cola Q1 2026 results](https://investors.coca-colacompany.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1158/coca-cola-reports-first-quarter-2026-results-and-updates-full-year-guidance)</cite>.

That is the bull case: KO is still compounding. The pushback is valuation. If a defensive consumer staple with 4%-5% organic revenue guide already trades at nearly 24x forward earnings, the entry price must be disciplined.

### Dividend / Tax Note

Coca-Cola declared a regular quarterly dividend of **$0.53/share**, annualized **$2.12/share** <cite>[Coca-Cola dividend declaration](https://www.coca-colacompany.com/media-center/board-of-directors-elects-two-new-officers-and-declares-regular-quarterly-dividend)</cite>. At $78.31, gross dividend yield is:

```text
gross yield = $2.12 / $78.31 = 2.71%
```

The U.S.-Thailand tax treaty technical explanation describes portfolio dividends as generally subject to a **15%** withholding rate under the treaty framework <cite>[IRS U.S.-Thailand treaty technical explanation](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/thaitech.pdf)</cite>. Rough net yield after 15% U.S. WHT is:

```text
net yield after 15% WHT ~= 2.71% x 85% = 2.30%
```

If a broker or DR process does not apply treaty relief and uses 30% U.S. withholding, net yield would be roughly:

```text
net yield after 30% WHT ~= 2.71% x 70% = 1.90%
```

ดังนั้น dividend ช่วย support แต่ไม่ใช่เหตุผลให้ไล่ซื้อ KO ที่ valuation premium. สำหรับ `KO80`, ต้องเช็ก issuer process, DR fees, withholding mechanics, FX conversion และเอกสารภาษีก่อนนับเป็น income จริง.

## Moat

- **Brand salience:** Coca-Cola, Sprite and Fanta remain global shelf and restaurant anchors.
- **Distribution system:** Bottling partners create physical availability at massive scale.
- **Concentrate economics:** Concentrate-heavy model keeps returns and margins higher than a pure bottler.
- **Portfolio breadth:** Sparkling is core, but KO can also compete in water, sports, coffee, tea, juice, dairy/plant-based and energy categories.
- **Pricing power:** Q1 2026 organic revenue growth came from price/mix and volume, showing pricing power has not vanished.
- **Dividend identity:** Long dividend record attracts defensive/income investors and supports valuation in risk-off markets.

## Key Risks

1. **Valuation compression:** If KO falls from 24x-25x earnings to 20x-22x, price downside can exceed a year of dividend income quickly.
2. **Volume elasticity:** Price/mix has limits. If consumers trade down or category volume weakens, organic growth can miss.
3. **FX:** International revenue is 60.1% of FY2025 net revenue, so USD strength can pressure reported numbers.
4. **Sugar, health and regulation:** Sugar taxes, package deposit rules, plastic waste, labeling and health concerns can cap growth or raise costs.
5. **Bottler execution:** The system is strong but not frictionless; bottler economics, local pricing and supply chain matter.
6. **Commodity / packaging costs:** Aluminum, PET, sweeteners and freight can pressure margins.
7. **Tax disputes / effective tax:** 10-K risk disclosures include tax matters and global regulatory uncertainty; do not assume reported tax rate is permanently smooth.
8. **DR execution risk:** `KO80` has D+N session, but quote, spread and premium/discount still need live checks.

## Valuation Notes

KO deserves a premium to lower-quality staples because brand, distribution and dividend durability are real. But paying almost 24x forward earnings for a business guiding 4%-5% organic revenue growth is a valuation choice, not a bargain.

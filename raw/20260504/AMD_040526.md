### Insider Activity

SECForm4 shows CTO Mark Papermaster sold 31,320 shares at $350 on 24 Apr 2026, 33,109 shares at $271.47 on 15 Apr 2026, and 3,293 shares at $225 on 6 Apr 2026; CEO Lisa Su sold 85,000 shares at $198.77 on 12 Mar 2026 and 125,000 shares at $214.36 on 11 Feb 2026 <cite>[SECForm4 AMD insider trading](https://www.secform4.com/insider-trading/2488.htm)</cite>. ### Balance Sheet and Supply Commitments

AMD had cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $10.6B at 27 Dec 2025, aggregate principal debt of $3.3B, and $3.0B available under an undrawn revolving credit facility <cite>[AMD FY2025 10-K/A](https://ir.amd.com/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000002488-26-000021/amd-20251227.htm)</cite>. The balance sheet is fine, but the operational bottleneck is supply chain, not leverage. FY2025 10-K/A discloses unconditional commitments of ~$12.2B, including $8.5B in fiscal 2026, mainly wafer/substrate purchases and cloud/service/software/technology commitments <cite>[AMD FY2025 10-K/A](https://ir.amd.com/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000002488-26-000021/amd-20251227.htm)</cite>.

Export control risk remains real: AMD recorded about $800M of inventory and related charges in Q2 2025 on MI308 Data Center GPU products due to U.S. export restrictions to China, later reversing about $360M in Q4 after some licenses were granted <cite>[AMD FY2025 10-K/A](https://ir.amd.com/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000002488-26-000021/amd-20251227.htm)</cite>. This matters because China restriction headlines can hit gross margin, inventory, customer mix and sentiment quickly.

## Moat

- **EPYC performance/share gains:** AMD has demonstrated sustained server CPU share gains and premium revenue share versus Intel.
- **Large hyperscaler validation:** OpenAI and Meta 6GW commitments validate AMD as a credible AI accelerator second source.
- **Full-stack AI ambition:** Instinct GPUs, EPYC CPUs, Pensando networking, ROCm and Helios rack-scale architecture create a broader platform story.
- **Fabless flexibility:** TSMC advanced-node access helps product competitiveness, though it also creates capacity dependence.
- **Xilinx/adaptive computing:** Embedded/adaptive portfolio gives AMD exposure outside pure PC/gaming cycles.

## Key Risks

1. **Valuation risk:** TTM P/E ~138x and forward P/E ~53x leave little tolerance for guide disappointment.
2. **Event risk:** Q1 FY2026 earnings are due 5 May 2026 after market close.
3. **AI GPU execution:** MI450/OpenAI/Meta revenue ramps mostly begin 2H 2026, so investors are paying ahead of proof.
4. **Nvidia ecosystem moat:** CUDA, networking, systems, software and developer inertia remain hard to displace.
5. **Supply constraints:** TSMC capacity, HBM, advanced packaging, substrates and rack deployment can cap upside even when demand exists.
6. **Export control / China risk:** MI308 charges and partial reversals show restrictions can hit inventory, margins and shipment timing.
7. **CPU competition:** Intel may recover some share if product and manufacturing execution improve.
8. **Customer concentration / bargaining power:** Hyperscaler orders are large but customers are sophisticated, price-sensitive and building internal silicon.
9. **Insider selling:** Selling-heavy recent Form 4 activity is not a thesis break, but it weakens the argument for chasing.
10. **DR execution risk:** AMD DRs have D+N access, but spreads/indicative values can widen around U.S. market news.

## Verdict

AMD เป็น "real company, real catalyst" ไม่ใช่ meme AI proxy. Data Center กลายเป็นเกือบครึ่งหนึ่งของรายได้ FY2025, EPYC share gains มีหลักฐาน, และ OpenAI/Meta 6GW deals ทำให้ AMD กลายเป็น second-source AI infrastructure candidate ที่ตลาดต้องจริงจัง.

แต่สำหรับ Mangkorn ที่เป็น moderate-risk investor: **อย่าไล่ซื้อก่อนงบที่ $340+**. Valuation ต้องการ execution ที่แม่นมากในปี 2026-2027 และ average analyst target ยังต่ำกว่าราคา spot. ให้รอ Q1 FY2026 วันที่ 5 พ.ค. 2026 แล้วค่อยตัดสินใจ. โซนแรกที่เริ่มสมเหตุสมผลคือ **$280-$300** หรือ AMD80 equivalent ประมาณ **฿1.81-฿1.94** ก่อน live quote/spread check. ถ้าตลาดให้ $240-$260 และ thesis ไม่เสีย ค่อยเพิ่ม. ถ้าหุ้นขึ้น $400-$430 ก่อน EPS upgrades ตามทัน ให้ trim แทนไล่.

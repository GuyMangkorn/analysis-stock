## 2. Position in Cycle

Cycle pattern: **Post-Earnings Paradox**

IBM is still in the same cycle bucket as the 10 May report: Q1 was operationally okay, but the stock keeps falling because the result did not settle the big debate. Is IBM a Software-led AI/hybrid-cloud compounder, or is it a legacy enterprise IT/consulting vendor whose economics get pressured by AI? The data says the first case is possible, but not yet proven.

The change is price. At $229-$230, IBM was a cautious starter. At $214-$219, the same thesis has better risk/reward, so the action moves to **buy the first tranche now**.

Pushback: do not confuse "down 30% from high" with "cheap enough for full size." If Software/Red Hat slows again, Consulting stays near 1% constant-currency growth, or FY2026 FCF guide gets cut, IBM can still trade toward a mid-teens multiple.

### Key Risks

1. **Valuation risk:** Current P/E around mid-30s is materially above the calculated 2016-2025 year-end P/E average of 25.2x. If growth normalizes, multiple compression can overwhelm EPS growth <cite>[StockAnalysis AAPL overview](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/)</cite> <cite>[CompaniesMarketCap AAPL P/E history](https://companiesmarketcap.com/apple/pe-ratio/)</cite>.
2. **Average-target upside is thin:** Consensus target $304.69 gives only about +1.9% upside from current quote, so fresh money has poor near-term risk/reward <cite>[StockAnalysis AAPL forecast](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/forecast/)</cite>.
3. **iPhone concentration:** iPhone was 51.3% of Q2 FY2026 net sales and 55.8% of H1 FY2026 net sales by calculation, so an iPhone cycle miss still matters a lot <cite>[Apple Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000013/aapl-20260328.htm)</cite>.
4. **Component and memory-cost pressure:** Apple’s 10-Q says supply constraints and increasing component costs, including advanced semiconductors, NAND and DRAM, could intensify and hurt demand, revenue, cost, gross margin and results <cite>[Apple Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000013/aapl-20260328.htm)</cite>.
5. **Tariff/geopolitical supply-chain risk:** Apple’s 10-Q warns tariffs and other measures can materially affect supply chain, components, pricing and gross margin; ultimate impact remains uncertain <cite>[Apple Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000013/aapl-20260328.htm)</cite>.
6. **App Store/regulatory risk:** In Epic-related proceedings, the Ninth Circuit upheld the 2025 injunction in part, modified some aspects, held Apple can charge a commission on link-out purchases, and remanded for further changes <cite>[Apple Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000013/aapl-20260328.htm)</cite>.
7. **AI/product-quality risk:** Apple’s risk factor says complex products and new AI features can increase safety risks, harmful or inaccurate content experiences, defects, vulnerabilities, quality problems, recalls and regulatory/litigation costs <cite>[Apple Q2 FY2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000013/aapl-20260328.htm)</cite>.

Ternus is product/hardware-heavy, which can be positive, but execution risk rises during the first CEO transition since Cook took over <cite>[Apple CEO transition release, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.apple.com/ca/newsroom/2026/04/tim-cook-to-become-apple-executive-chairman-john-ternus-to-become-apple-ceo/)</cite>.

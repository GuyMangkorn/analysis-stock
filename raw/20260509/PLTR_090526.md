## Business Model

Palantir is an operational-AI software company serving government and commercial customers. The investable question is whether AIP and Ontology turn PLTR from a complex government/data-integration vendor into a broad operating system for AI-driven institutions.

| Engine | Role | Investment importance |
|---|---|---|
| Gotham | Government/defense/intelligence workflows | Geopolitical demand and sticky mission-critical deployments |
| Foundry | Commercial operating data/workflow platform | U.S. commercial hypergrowth engine |
| AIP | AI application, agent and workflow layer | Main multiple driver |
| Apollo | Software deployment and operations across environments | Supports edge/on-prem/regulated deployments |
| Ontology | Data/logic/action/security layer | Core differentiation if deeply embedded |
| FDE model | Human implementation engine | Helps win hard deployments; must scale efficiently |

## Moat

- **Mission-critical workflows:** Government and defense use cases can be very sticky once embedded.
- **AIP + Ontology architecture:** Palantir is trying to own the operational layer where AI models meet enterprise/government data, permissions and actions.
- **Customer expansion:** Net dollar retention was **150%** in Q1 2026, indicating strong expansion among existing customers <cite>[Palantir Q1 2026 Business Update](https://investors.palantir.com/files/Palantir%20-%20Q1%202026%20Business%20Update.pdf)</cite>.
- **Commercial acceleration:** U.S. commercial revenue grew **133% YoY** in Q1 2026, and U.S. commercial RDV grew **112% YoY** to **$4.92B** <cite>[Palantir Q1 2026 Business Update](https://investors.palantir.com/files/Palantir%20-%20Q1%202026%20Business%20Update.pdf)</cite>.
- **Government credibility:** U.S. government revenue grew **84% YoY** in Q1 2026, supporting the defense/geopolitical thesis <cite>[Palantir Q1 2026 Business Update](https://investors.palantir.com/files/Palantir%20-%20Q1%202026%20Business%20Update.pdf)</cite>.
- **Balance sheet and FCF:** $8.0B cash/Treasuries and no debt, plus Q1 adjusted FCF of **$924.6M** <cite>[Palantir Q1 2026 Business Update](https://investors.palantir.com/files/Palantir%20-%20Q1%202026%20Business%20Update.pdf)</cite>.

## Key Risks

1. **High-multiple drawdown risk:** A good Q1 did not prevent post-earnings weakness, which shows how much was priced in.
2. **AI platform commoditization:** If Microsoft/cloud vendors/model labs bundle enough operational AI, PLTR may face pricing pressure.
3. **Political/privacy scrutiny:** Government data/AI work can trigger backlash, lawsuits, procurement pressure or reputational risk.
4. **Contract concentration/procurement cycles:** Large government and enterprise deals have long cycles, and timing can be uneven <cite>[Palantir FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1321655/000132165526000011/pltr-20251231.htm)</cite>.
5. **Insider selling-plan optics:** 10b5-1 plans and Form 4 selling summaries reduce comfort for aggressive buying.
6. **Stock-based compensation:** Adjusted metrics add back meaningful SBC; watch share count and quality of EPS.
7. **DR tracking risk:** PLTR DR quotes are stale in project snapshot and must be checked live.

### Business Model

### Key Risks

1. **Valuation is not cheap on bank metrics:** current P/E ~15 looks low, but current price divided by Q1 TBVPS implies ~2.9x tangible book value; that is a premium multiple for a bank and requires sustained high ROTCE.
2. **Credit cycle risk:** Q1 2026 credit costs were $2.5B and Card Services net charge-off rate was 3.47%; if unemployment/consumer stress rises, EPS can fall quickly <cite>[JPMorgan Chase 1Q26 earnings presentation](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2026/1st-quarter/ba305358-f754-4f76-a59d-5278b3bcf99a.pdf)</cite>.
3. **NII / rate path risk:** management expects FY2026 net interest income of about $103B and NII excluding Markets of about $95B, market dependent; lower rates or deposit-cost pressure could cap earnings <cite>[JPMorgan Chase 1Q26 earnings presentation](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2026/1st-quarter/ba305358-f754-4f76-a59d-5278b3bcf99a.pdf)</cite>.

### Why P/E Is Only Around 15

This is the core question. The simple answer: **banks deserve lower P/E than asset-light compounders because they need capital, carry credit risk, and cannot scale earnings without balance-sheet risk**.

More specifically:

| Reason | What it means for JPM |
|---|---|
| Capital intensity | A bank must hold CET1 capital against assets/RWA. JPM's Q1 standardized RWA was about $2.0T and CET1 ratio was 14.3%, so earnings cannot be levered freely like software revenue <cite>[JPMorgan Chase 1Q26 earnings presentation](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2026/1st-quarter/ba305358-f754-4f76-a59d-5278b3bcf99a.pdf)</cite> |
| Cyclicality | Credit costs, trading revenue, IB fees and loan growth move with the cycle; Q1 was strong, but not all revenue streams are equally recurring |
| Regulation | GSIB/Basel/capital rules can convert business strength into higher required capital instead of higher shareholder payout |
| Lower terminal growth | A $4.9T-asset bank cannot compound revenue like a smaller software company; long-term value comes from ROTCE, buybacks, dividends and disciplined risk <cite>[JPMorgan Chase 1Q26 earnings presentation](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2026/1st-quarter/ba305358-f754-4f76-a59d-5278b3bcf99a.pdf)</cite> |
| P/TBV math | Calculation: $315.73 / $108.87 TBVPS = 2.90x P/TBV. If sustainable ROTCE is 20%, then implied P/E is roughly 2.90 / 0.20 = 14.5x. So P/E 15 is actually consistent with a premium bank multiple, not a distressed multiple <cite>[StockAnalysis JPM quote](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jpm/)</cite> <cite>[JPMorgan Chase 1Q26 earnings presentation](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2026/1st-quarter/ba305358-f754-4f76-a59d-5278b3bcf99a.pdf)</cite> |
| Earnings quality discount | FY2026 EPS estimates may include strong markets/IB and rate conditions. If EPS normalizes, the "E" in P/E falls and true multiple rises <cite>[StockAnalysis JPM forecast](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jpm/forecast/)</cite> <cite>[JPMorgan Chase 1Q26 earnings presentation](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2026/1st-quarter/ba305358-f754-4f76-a59d-5278b3bcf99a.pdf)</cite> |

Bottom line: **P/E 15 is low only if compared with tech. For a bank, JPM at ~2.9x tangible book and above its 10-year P/E median is already pricing in high quality.**

## Long-Term Outlook

ระยะยาว JPM ยังดูดีในฐานะ **core-quality U.S. financial compounder**:

- Deposit scale, payments, IB, markets, custody and wealth create multiple engines rather than one lending book.
- AWM can compound with market levels and net inflows; Q1 2026 AUM was $4.789T and client assets $7.103T <cite>[JPMorgan Chase 1Q26 earnings presentation](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2026/1st-quarter/ba305358-f754-4f76-a59d-5278b3bcf99a.pdf)</cite>.
- CIB/payments franchise remains globally important; Q1 2026 Banking & Payments revenue was $10.425B and Markets & Securities Services revenue was $12.954B <cite>[JPMorgan Chase 1Q26 earnings presentation](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2026/1st-quarter/ba305358-f754-4f76-a59d-5278b3bcf99a.pdf)</cite>.
- Capital return supports per-share growth if bought back at reasonable prices.

แต่ long-term return จากจุดนี้น่าจะมาจาก **EPS growth + dividend + buyback** มากกว่า multiple expansion. ถ้า JPM sustain ROTCE high-teens/20% ได้ หุ้นควร compound ได้ดี. ถ้า credit cycle, capital rules, NII compression หรือ succession uncertainty ทำให้ ROTCE reset ลงไป mid-teens, P/TBV 2.9x จะดูแพงทันที.

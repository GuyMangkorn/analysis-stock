### Key Risks

1. **Expectation risk:** Q1 FY2027 guide and consensus are already extremely high; a normal beat may not be enough to move the stock if guidance is not exceptional.
2. **Valuation risk:** At $235.74, average analyst target upside is only 15.61% and stock trades near the 52-week high <cite>[StockAnalysis NVDA forecast](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/forecast/)</cite>.
3. **China risk:** Q1 guide excludes Data Center compute revenue from China; Reuters reported H200 approvals but no deliveries as of May 14, 2026 <cite>[NVIDIA Q4 FY2026 earnings release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2026/)</cite> <cite>[Investing.com / Reuters, 14 May 2026](https://m.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/exclusiveus-clears-h200-chip-sales-to-10-china-firms-nvidia-ceo-looks-for-breakthrough-4687123?ampMode=1)</cite>.
4. **Customer concentration:** One direct customer represented 22% and another 14% of FY2026 revenue; both were primarily Compute & Networking <cite>[NVIDIA FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)</cite>.
5. **Gross margin risk:** Q1 guide implies around 75% gross margin; any lower guide could force multiple compression <cite>[NVIDIA Q4 FY2026 earnings release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2026/)</cite>.
6. **Supply-chain risk:** TSMC, HBM, advanced packaging, racks, power/cooling and data-center deployment timing can bottleneck revenue recognition.
7. **Hyperscaler custom silicon:** Google, AWS, Microsoft and others have economic reasons to shift some workloads to in-house silicon.
8. **Insider selling optics:** MarketBeat reports heavy 12-month insider selling and no insider buying; not a standalone sell signal, but a caution near highs <cite>[MarketBeat NVDA insider trades](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NASDAQ/NVDA/insider-trades/)</cite>.
9. **SET DR premium/spread risk:** Multiple NVDA DRs exist, but live indicative value must be checked because stale bid/offer can be far from U.S. close.

### Valuation Pushback

NVDA is not obviously "cheap" even though forward P/E looks less scary than TTM P/E. Current P/E is **48.11x** and forward P/E **28.13x** at StockAnalysis <cite>[StockAnalysis NVDA overview](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/)</cite>. CompaniesMarketCap shows current TTM P/E around **47.8x**, while visible 2015-2025 year-end P/E observations average around **54.2x** by calculation; this says current TTM multiple is not unprecedented for NVDA, but it does **not** prove downside is low because earnings are at a cyclical/structural acceleration point <cite>[CompaniesMarketCap NVDA P/E history](https://companiesmarketcap.com/nvidia/pe-ratio/)</cite>.

The market is paying for:

- Q1 FY2027 revenue near or above **$78B**.
- Q2 guide likely well above Q1.
- Blackwell ramp and Rubin visibility.
- Hyperscaler capex acceleration.
- China optionality not currently in company base guide.

If Q1 results are strong but Q2 guide is merely in-line, the stock can still sell off. That is why action is **Wait**, not "buy before call".

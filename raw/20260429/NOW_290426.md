### Business Reality

ServiceNow builds a cloud-based AI/workflow platform for enterprise operations. The company says its platform connects people, processes, and data, and its products are grouped into four areas: **Technology, CRM and Industry, Core Business, and Creator and Other** <cite>[ServiceNow FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371526000007/now-20251231.htm)</cite>. Core product surfaces include ITSM, ITOM, risk, customer service, field service, sales/order management, HR service delivery, workplace service delivery, creator tools, and AI agents <cite>[ServiceNow FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371526000007/now-20251231.htm)</cite>.

Revenue quality is high. Subscription revenue is the main engine: FY2025 subscription revenue was $12.883B out of total revenue $13.278B, or about **97.0%** of total revenue <cite>[ServiceNow FY2025 results](https://investor.servicenow.com/news/news-details/2026/ServiceNow-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Financial-Results-Board-of-Directors-Authorizes-Additional-5B-for-Share-Repurchase-Program/default.aspx)</cite>. Subscription revenues are generally recognized ratably over the contract term for cloud offerings; contracts are typically fixed-consideration, non-cancellable, and invoiced annually in advance <cite>[ServiceNow FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371526000007/now-20251231.htm)</cite>.

Geographic mix in FY2025 was North America $8.348B, EMEA $3.402B, and Asia Pacific/other $1.528B, implying roughly 62.9% / 25.6% / 11.5% of revenue respectively <cite>[ServiceNow FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371526000007/now-20251231.htm)</cite>. Revenue outside North America represented 37% of total revenues in 2025 and 2024 <cite>[ServiceNow FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371526000007/now-20251231.htm)</cite>.

### Competitive Position

ServiceNow is still a high-quality enterprise software platform with strong switching cost, deep workflow context, and high subscription mix. The company argues that AI models can generate insights, but enterprises still need governance, workflow orchestration, data integrity, and execution across systems of record; this is the layer where ServiceNow wants to sit <cite>[ServiceNow FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371526000007/now-20251231.htm)</cite>.

Main competitive pressure comes from:

| Competitor set | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Salesforce / Microsoft / Oracle / SAP | Systems-of-record and CRM/platform incumbents can bundle workflow, AI agents, and data access. |
| Atlassian / BMC / Freshworks / Zendesk-like tools | ITSM and service-management alternatives can attack from lower price points. |
| Workday / HR suites | HR workflow overlap. |
| AI-native app builders | Risk that customers build lightweight workflow apps without paying premium ServiceNow seat/platform pricing. |
| Consulting/SI ecosystems | Can either accelerate ServiceNow adoption or steer clients toward competing stacks. |

Exact market share was **not verified from a credible current source**. ServiceNow cites recognition as a leader in analyst reports, including Forrester and IDC MarketScape categories, but those are positioning signals, not clean market-share percentages <cite>[ServiceNow Q1 2026 results](https://investor.servicenow.com/news/news-details/2026/ServiceNow-Reports-First-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)</cite>.

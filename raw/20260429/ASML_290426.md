### Business Quality

ASML คือ supplier สำคัญของ semiconductor ecosystem โดยขาย hardware, software และ services ที่ช่วย chipmakers ผลิต pattern ของ integrated circuits <cite>[ASML Q1 2026 results](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/q1-2026-financial-results)</cite>. จุดแข็งคือ EUV lithography ซึ่งแทบเป็น chokepoint สำหรับ leading-edge logic และ advanced DRAM; ลูกค้ารายใหญ่เช่น TSMC, Samsung, SK Hynix, Intel ต้องพึ่งเครื่องจักรระดับนี้เพื่อขยับ process node.

ปี 2025 ASML ทำ total net sales **€32.7B**, gross margin **52.8%**, net income **€9.6B**, EPS basic **€24.73**, และ R&D **€4.7B** <cite>[ASML 2025 Annual Report](https://www.asml.com/en/investors/annual-report/2025)</cite>. ระบบที่ขายในปี 2025 รวม **535 units** แบ่งเป็น **48 EUV**, **279 DUV**, และ **208 metrology/inspection systems** <cite>[ASML 2025 Annual Report](https://www.asml.com/en/investors/annual-report/2025)</cite>.

Q1 2026 ยังคงแข็ง: total net sales **€8.767B**, gross margin **53.0%**, net income **€2.757B**, EPS basic **€7.15** <cite>[ASML Q1 2026 results](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/q1-2026-financial-results)</cite>. บริษัทเพิ่ม FY2026 guidance เป็น revenue **€36B-€40B** และ gross margin **51%-53%** จากแรงหนุน AI infrastructure และ customer capacity expansion <cite>[ASML Q1 2026 results](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/q1-2026-financial-results)</cite>.

## Business Model

ASML sells lithography systems and related services to semiconductor manufacturers. Revenue buckets:

- **EUV systems:** core chokepoint for advanced logic and advanced DRAM. This is where ASML's monopoly-like economics matter most.
- **DUV systems:** broader mature/mainstream node demand, including China exposure.
- **Metrology and inspection:** measurement/overlay control tools that improve yield.
- **Installed Base Management:** service, field options, upgrades, productivity improvements. This business reached **€8.193B** in 2025 vs **€6.494B** in 2024 <cite>[ASML Q4 2025 results](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/q4-2025-financial-results)</cite>.

The best part of the model is customer lock-in. Once a fab process is built around ASML tools, service and upgrades become mission-critical. The hard part is cyclicality: even monopoly suppliers feel pain when semiconductor capex pauses or when export rules limit shipments.

## Moat

| Moat source | Strength | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| EUV technology leadership | Very high | EUV systems are essential for leading-edge nodes; competitors cannot easily replicate decades of optics, source, stage, software, and supply-chain integration. |
| Ecosystem dependency | Very high | TSMC/Samsung/Intel/SK Hynix roadmaps require ASML coordination years ahead. |
| Installed base | High | Field upgrades and services provide recurring-like support revenue and deepen switching costs. |
| R&D scale | High | 2025 R&D was €4.7B, reinforcing technology gap <cite>[ASML 2025 Annual Report](https://www.asml.com/en/investors/annual-report/2025)</cite>. |
| Supply chain complexity | High but also risk | Unique suppliers protect the moat but create bottleneck and geopolitical exposure. |

## Key Risks

1. **Valuation compression:** If P/E reverts from ~48x-50x toward 35x while EPS estimates stay unchanged, downside to $1,300 or lower is plausible.
2. **Export controls / China:** ASML explicitly includes export-control outcomes in 2026 guidance bandwidth <cite>[ASML Q1 2026 results](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/q1-2026-financial-results)</cite>. China DUV was stronger than anticipated in 2025, but this also raises future normalization risk <cite>[ASML 2025 Annual Report financials](https://www.asml.com/en/investors/annual-report/2025/financials)</cite>.
3. **High-NA adoption timing:** TSMC caution on High-NA cost can delay the most bullish narrative, even if the long-term roadmap remains intact <cite>[MarketWatch](https://www.marketwatch.com/story/analysts-try-to-quell-the-panic-after-taiwan-semiconductor-said-a-high-end-asml-machine-was-too-expensive-e6e43f6e)</cite>.
4. **Customer concentration and capex cycles:** ASML sells to a small number of massive chipmakers. If TSMC/Samsung/Intel/SK Hynix adjust capex timing, ASML order cadence can move sharply.
5. **FX and DR execution:** Thai investor returns through ASML01 depend on underlying ADR/share movement, USD/THB or EUR/THB effects, and local spread/liquidity.

### Business Quality

Tencent รายงาน FY2025 revenue **RMB751.8B**, เพิ่ม **14% YoY**; gross profit **RMB422.6B**, เพิ่ม **21% YoY**; non-IFRS operating profit **RMB280.7B**, เพิ่ม **18% YoY** <cite>[Tencent 4Q25/FY25 results release](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/03/18/e6a646796d0d869acc76271c9ee1a6a5.pdf)</cite>. นี่เป็น quality growth ที่สำคัญกว่า revenue growth ธรรมดา เพราะ gross margin ขยับขึ้นจาก mix ที่ดีขึ้นใน games, marketing services, fintech/cloud efficiency.

Segment หลักยังแข็ง:

- **VAS** revenue FY2025 **RMB369.3B**, โต **16% YoY**; Domestic Games โต **18%**, International Games โต **33%** <cite>[Tencent 2025 Annual Report](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/04/09/62d786fcf3d3c8cb7e54791ee95439ac.pdf)</cite>.
- **Marketing Services** revenue **RMB145.0B**, โต **19% YoY** จาก AI-powered ad targeting, Video Accounts, Weixin Search และ closed-loop ads <cite>[Tencent 2025 Annual Report](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/04/09/62d786fcf3d3c8cb7e54791ee95439ac.pdf)</cite>.
- **FinTech and Business Services** revenue **RMB229.4B**, โต **8% YoY**; Business Services revenue grew high-teens จาก cloud/AI demand และ eCommerce tech service fees <cite>[Tencent 2025 Annual Report](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/04/09/62d786fcf3d3c8cb7e54791ee95439ac.pdf)</cite>.
- Weixin + WeChat combined MAU **1.418B** at 31 Dec 2025, เพิ่ม **2% YoY**; ecosystem ยังเป็น distribution moat ที่ยากมากสำหรับคู่แข่ง <cite>[Tencent 2025 Annual Report](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/04/09/62d786fcf3d3c8cb7e54791ee95439ac.pdf)</cite>.

จุดที่ต้อง push back คือ AI spend. Tencent disclosed ว่า costs/expenses สำหรับ new AI products อยู่ที่ **RMB18B ในปี 2025** และบริษัทคาดว่าจะ **more than double investments in New AI Products in 2026** <cite>[Tencent 4Q25/FY25 presentation](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/03/18/2804dbdae364ca25b82d21bc8304f1d3.pdf)</cite>. นี่ทำให้ short-term earnings upside อาจถูก cap แม้ core businesses แข็งแรง.

## Moat

- **Network effects:** Weixin/WeChat 1.418B MAU creates unmatched distribution and social graph in China <cite>[Tencent 2025 Annual Report](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/04/09/62d786fcf3d3c8cb7e54791ee95439ac.pdf)</cite>.
- **Evergreen game IP:** Domestic and international games are now growing again, with AI helping content production and engagement.
- **Ad data loop:** Video Accounts, Weixin Search, Mini Programs and closed-loop ads let Tencent improve targeting without simply raising ad load.
- **FinTech rails:** Payments and licensed financial partnerships give Tencent transactional data, merchant relationships and daily utility.
- **Balance sheet and cash generation:** FY2025 free cash flow **RMB182.6B**, net cash **RMB107.1B** <cite>[Tencent 4Q25/FY25 results release](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/03/18/e6a646796d0d869acc76271c9ee1a6a5.pdf)</cite>.
- **AI integration surface:** Tencent can embed AI into Weixin, games, ads, cloud, meeting/enterprise tools and agents instead of relying only on standalone chatbot adoption.

## Key Risks

1. **AI investment drag:** New AI Products investment is expected to more than double in 2026; monetization may lag spending <cite>[Tencent 4Q25/FY25 presentation](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/03/18/2804dbdae364ca25b82d21bc8304f1d3.pdf)</cite>.
2. **Buyback intensity may fall:** 2025 buyback was HK$80.0B, but management signaled lower buyback value versus 2025 to fund AI investments <cite>[Tencent 2025 Annual Report](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/04/09/62d786fcf3d3c8cb7e54791ee95439ac.pdf)</cite> <cite>[Tencent 4Q25/FY25 presentation](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/03/18/2804dbdae364ca25b82d21bc8304f1d3.pdf)</cite>.
3. **China platform regulation:** VIE/structure-contract exposure, content regulation, game approvals, fintech rules and data rules can cap multiple.

5. **Gaming cyclicality:** Delta Force/VALORANT/Wuthering Waves momentum must persist; a miss in game pipeline can quickly affect sentiment.

ThaiBev เป็น ASEAN beverage platform ที่มีแกนกำไรจาก spirits, beer, non-alcoholic beverages และ food. FY2025 revenue from sale of goods อยู่ที่ **THB333.3B**, ลดจาก **THB340.3B** ใน FY2024; profit attributable to owners อยู่ที่ **THB25.36B**, ลดจาก **THB27.22B** <cite>[ThaiBev FY2025 financial statements](https://investor.thaibev.com/storage/download/FS/20251126-thaibev-fs-fy2025-en.pdf)</cite>. นี่ไม่ใช่ภาพ growth acceleration แต่เป็น defensive cash flow ที่ตลาด de-rate.

Spirits revenue โต **3.5%** และ EBITDA โต **6.9%**; Beer revenue ลด **14.4%** แต่ EBITDA โต **4.7%** เพราะ material cost และ production efficiency ดีขึ้น; NAB และ Food ยังอ่อน <cite>[ThaiBev 1QFY26 Business Update](https://links.sgx.com/1.0.0/corporate-announcements/KCYEGYVJGMRUTMZC/875169_Business%20Update_1Q26_EN.pdf)</cite>.

### Valuation Pushback

The easy bull case is: "P/E 10.6x, dividend yield near 6%, target upside 34%, buy." That is too simple.

The pushback:

1. FY2025 revenue and owner profit declined, so low P/E may be a value trap if earnings keep shrinking <cite>[ThaiBev FY2025 financial statements](https://investor.thaibev.com/storage/download/FS/20251126-thaibev-fs-fy2025-en.pdf)</cite>.
2. 1QFY26 EBITDA improvement came despite falling revenue; if cost tailwinds fade, operating leverage could reverse <cite>[ThaiBev 1QFY26 Business Update](https://links.sgx.com/1.0.0/corporate-announcements/KCYEGYVJGMRUTMZC/875169_Business%20Update_1Q26_EN.pdf)</cite>.
3. Net interest-bearing debt to EBITDA was **3.24x** at 31 Dec 2025, not dangerous for an infrastructure-like beverage cash flow, but high enough to cap aggressive equity multiple expansion <cite>[ThaiBev 1QFY26 Business Update](https://links.sgx.com/1.0.0/corporate-announcements/KCYEGYVJGMRUTMZC/875169_Business%20Update_1Q26_EN.pdf)</cite>.
4. Beer/Vietnam is still the sore spot: Beer sales fell 14.4% in 1QFY26, affected by subdued Thailand demand, severe weather in Vietnam and unfavorable VND translation <cite>[ThaiBev 1QFY26 Business Update](https://links.sgx.com/1.0.0/corporate-announcements/KCYEGYVJGMRUTMZC/875169_Business%20Update_1Q26_EN.pdf)</cite>.
5. Dividend yield is attractive, but Thailand dividends are generally subject to 10% WHT for individuals/residents and non-residents; DR pass-through economics may include issuer process/fees <cite>[PwC Thailand withholding taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/corporate/withholding-taxes)</cite> <cite>[PwC Thailand individual income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/individual/income-determination)</cite>.

Conclusion: valuation is attractive enough for a **starter**, not attractive enough to ignore execution proof.

This is **not** a clean Recovery yet because revenue is still declining. It is **Correction + Catalyst**: buy only where price compensates for execution risk.

### Dividend / Tax Note

StockAnalysis shows annual dividend **S$0.025/share** and yield **5.73%** at its S$0.425 snapshot <cite>[StockAnalysis SGX:Y92 dividend](https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sgx/Y92/dividend/)</cite>. Because ThaiBev is incorporated in Thailand, Thai dividend WHT is materially relevant: PwC Thailand lists dividend WHT for resident individuals at **10%**, and non-resident dividends generally at **10%** unless treaty rules apply <cite>[PwC Thailand withholding taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/corporate/withholding-taxes)</cite> <cite>[PwC Thailand individual income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/individual/income-determination)</cite>. Rough net yield after 10% WHT before DR issuer fees/tax remittance complexity: **~5.2%-5.4%**.

For Mangkorn: dividend yield is attractive, but do not treat gross yield as final cash yield. For THAIBEV19, confirm issuer treatment, fees, payment timing, and Thai tax handling before relying on it as income.

## Moat

- **Scale and distribution:** Thailand and ASEAN routes-to-market remain a real advantage.
- **Brand portfolio:** SangSom, Mekhong, Chang, Oishi, est, Crystal, F&N assets, and Sabeco brands create a wide consumer shelf presence.
- **Cash flow resilience:** Even with weak revenue, 1QFY26 EBITDA still grew 1.8% YoY.
- **Spirits economics:** Spirits generated only 35.6% of FY2025 sales revenue but contributed THB19.17B of profit attributable to owners, far above other segments <cite>[ThaiBev FY2025 financial statements](https://investor.thaibev.com/storage/download/FS/20251126-thaibev-fs-fy2025-en.pdf)</cite>.
- **Regional optionality:** Vietnam/Sabeco is currently a problem, but stabilization would be a meaningful catalyst because Beer is the largest sales segment.

## Key Risks

1. **Revenue decline:** FY2025 sales revenue and 1QFY26 sales revenue both declined.
2. **Beer / Vietnam weakness:** Beer revenue fell 14.4% in 1QFY26, with Vietnam weather, VND translation and demand pressure cited by management.
3. **Leverage:** Net interest-bearing debt to EBITDA was 3.24x at 31 Dec 2025.
4. **Dividend sustainability:** Yield is attractive, but if earnings/FCF weaken or refinancing costs stay high, payout comfort can compress.
5. **Regulation and social pressure:** Alcohol excise, advertising restrictions, drink-driving rules, market liberalization, and ESG exclusion can cap valuation.
6. **Competition:** Thai beer, white spirits, energy drinks, RTD tea, and Vietnam beer are all competitive categories.
7. **FX:** VND/THB, SGD/THB and DR pricing can create unexpected return gaps for Thai investors.
8. **DR liquidity/premium risk:** THAIBEV19 is D-only with bid/offer spread around 0.93% in the project snapshot; avoid market orders.
